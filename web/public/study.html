Temporal drift comparison

Resample sequences, preserve their shared errors.

360 paired sequences

On unseen drift, temporal CRC has a +2.50 percentage-point difference in sequence false release. The interval includes zero.

Method

The analysis resamples both selectors together at the sequence level within each family. Coverage and correct accepted frames are reported alongside false release; discarding every frame therefore has zero measured utility.

12 dependent frames → sequence loss → family strata → 10,000 paired resamples
